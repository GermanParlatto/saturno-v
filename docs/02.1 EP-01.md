# Asset E0-01 · Cómic «La señal» — Guía de Prompts
## Proyecto Waku-Code · Documento de producción
### 5 paneles · Framework: Sujeto + Contexto + Visual + Estilo

---

## Instrucciones generales

1. **Herramienta**: Gemini Pro 3.1
2. **Imagen de referencia**: subir `spoky-frontal-canonico.png` junto al prompt en los paneles donde Spoky aparece (Paneles 2, 4, 5). En Panel 1 y 3 no es necesario (Spoky no aparece o no se ve su cara).
3. **Vista de referencia adicional**: en Panel 2 usar `spoky-espaldas.png`, en Panel 4 no se requiere referencia de personaje (solo silueta genérica), en Panel 5 usar `spoky-frontal-canonico.png`.
4. **Texto**: NO incluir texto en el prompt. Todo texto narrativo y burbujas de diálogo se añaden en post-producción (Canva).
5. **Formato de cada panel**: orientación horizontal o cuadrada, ~1080 × 960 px por panel. Los 5 paneles se ensamblan verticalmente en Canva para formar la tira webtoon final de 1080 × ~4800 px.
6. **Variantes**: generar 3-4 variantes de cada panel y seleccionar la mejor.

## Panel 1 · "La nave estrellada" ✅ APROBADO

**Narrativa**: plano general, noche, la Waku-Go estrellada en un campo terrestre.
**Texto en post-prod**: "En algún lugar de la Tierra… algo cayó del cielo."
**Referencia de Spoky**: no necesaria (Spoky no aparece).

```
MAIN SUBJECT:
A small damaged spaceship (the Waku-Go) crashed in an open field.
The ship is compact and rounded, pixel art style, with visible
cracks, a broken antenna, and thin wisps of smoke rising from it.
The ship has small glowing green lights flickering on its hull,
suggesting it still has some power. No characters visible — the
ship is the focal point.
Viewing angle: wide establishing shot, slightly elevated camera
looking down at the crash site from a distance.

CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT:
An open grassy field on Earth at night. A few silhouette-style
pixel art trees frame the edges of the scene. The sky is dark
blue-black filled with pixel stars. A faint impact trail (subtle
scorched earth line) leads to where the ship rests, suggesting
it skidded on landing.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
Colors: dark blue sky (#0D1B2A), deep teal (#1B2838) for distant
trees, muted green for grass, neon green (#7FFF00) accents on
the ship's flickering lights. The smoke is light gray, thin.
Lighting: nighttime — the only light sources are the stars above
and the faint green glow from the damaged ship. The glow casts
a subtle green halo on the grass immediately around the ship.
Mood: mysterious, quiet, something just happened.

STYLE AND AESTHETICS:
Pixel art style — clean blocky pixels, visible pixel grid,
no anti-aliasing, no smoothing. Limited color palette (max 8
colors). Retro 16-bit to 32-bit game aesthetic, similar to
classic SNES/GBA cutscene backgrounds. Inspired by pixel art
scenes from games like Celeste, Hyper Light Drifter, or
Owlboy — atmospheric pixel landscapes with mood lighting.

FORMAT: Single panel illustration, horizontal orientation
(1080 x 960 px). No text, no speech bubbles, no UI elements.
```

## Panel 2 · "Spoky enciende el radar"

**Narrativa**: interior oscuro de la nave, Spoky de espaldas frente al panel de control, una pantalla se enciende.
**Texto en post-prod**: Spoky (pensamiento): "La nave no puede despegar sin tripulación…"
**Referencia de Spoky**: subir `spoky-espaldas.png` como imagen de referencia.

```
MAIN SUBJECT:
Based on this character reference — a small neon green pixel art
alien (Spoky) seen from behind, standing in front of a large
spaceship control panel. Spoky is small compared to the console,
emphasizing that he is alone in a big ship. His two zigzag
antennas are visible from behind, silhouetted against the glow
of the screen.
Viewing angle: medium shot from behind Spoky, slightly above
shoulder level, looking at the control panel with him.

CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT:
Interior of a damaged spaceship cockpit. Everything is dark and
powered down — black screens, dead buttons, no lights — EXCEPT
for one circular screen on the right side of the console that
has just turned on. This screen displays a green radar sweep
pattern (a rotating line inside a circle). The radar screen is
the only light source in the entire scene. Faint sparks or
loose cables hang from the ceiling suggesting damage.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
Colors: very dark interior — near black (#0A0A12) for walls and
ceiling, dark gray (#1A1A2E) for the console panels. The ONLY
color accent is the neon green (#7FFF00) radar screen glow,
which casts green light on Spoky's back, the edges of the
console, and the floor immediately around him.
Lighting: extreme contrast — almost entirely dark except for the
single radar screen. The green glow creates dramatic rim lighting
on Spoky's silhouette. Mood: lonely, determined, a spark of hope
in the darkness.

STYLE AND AESTHETICS:
Pixel art style — clean blocky pixels, visible pixel grid,
no anti-aliasing, no smoothing. Limited color palette (max 6
colors — mostly dark with green accent). Retro 16-bit to 32-bit
game aesthetic. Inspired by cockpit scenes from Hyper Light
Drifter and FTL: Faster Than Light — moody interiors with
single light source pixel art. The contrast between darkness
and the radar glow is the visual hook of this panel.

FORMAT: Single panel illustration, horizontal orientation
(1080 x 960 px). No text, no speech bubbles, no UI elements.
```

## Panel 3 · "El radar barre la Tierra"

**Narrativa**: vista cenital del planeta Tierra, un barrido de radar verde gira sobre la superficie.
**Texto en post-prod**: "El Radar de Potencial barrió el planeta entero…"
**Referencia de Spoky**: no necesaria (Spoky no aparece).

```
MAIN SUBJECT:
Planet Earth seen from space, displayed as a pixel art radar
screen. The planet is centered, showing pixelated continents
(simplified blocky shapes — recognizable but stylized). A bright
neon green radar sweep line rotates clockwise from the center,
leaving a fading green trail behind it. The sweep line has just
passed over one of the continents. Small dot clusters scattered
across the surface represent population centers, but none are
highlighted yet.
Viewing angle: top-down orbital view, looking straight down
at Earth.

CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT:
Deep space surrounding the planet — pure dark blue-black (#0A0A14)
with scattered pixel stars. The Earth floats in the center of the
composition. The image looks like it is being displayed on a
circular radar screen inside the Waku-Go — subtle circular scan
lines or a faint circular grid overlay the planet, suggesting
this is what Spoky sees on his radar monitor, not a photograph.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
Colors: dark space background (#0A0A14), Earth in muted blues
(#1A3A5C) for oceans and muted greens (#2D5A27) for continents.
The radar sweep line and its trail are bright neon green (#7FFF00).
Faint circular grid lines in dark green (#1A4A1A). Small white
dots for stars and population clusters.
Lighting: the radar sweep line illuminates the area it passes
over with a brief green glow. The rest of the planet is dimly
lit. Mood: searching, scanning, anticipation.

STYLE AND AESTHETICS:
Pixel art style — clean blocky pixels, visible pixel grid,
no anti-aliasing, no smoothing. Limited color palette (max 6
colors). This should look like a retro game radar/map screen —
inspired by the world maps from classic RPGs like Chrono Trigger
or EarthBound, combined with a military radar aesthetic. The
pixelated continents should be recognizable but simplified to
~20-30 pixel blocks each.

FORMAT: Single panel illustration, square orientation
(1080 x 1080 px — slightly larger to emphasize the planetary
scale). No text, no speech bubbles, no UI elements.
```

## Panel 4 · "La señal"

**Narrativa**: el radar se detiene, un punto brillante parpadea, zoom a una silueta humana genérica brillando.
**Texto en post-prod**: "…y se detuvo en una señal."
**Referencia de Spoky**: no necesaria (Spoky no aparece — silueta humana es genérica).

```
MAIN SUBJECT:
A close-up view of the radar screen showing a single bright
pulsing point of light. The point is distinctly brighter and
larger than any other dot on the screen — it radiates concentric
rings of green light like a sonar ping. Below or next to the
radar view, there is a small pixel art silhouette of a human
child/teenager — gender neutral, no specific features, just a
glowing outline made of neon green pixels. The silhouette stands
with a subtle glow around it, suggesting potential/energy.
Viewing angle: close-up on the radar screen, as if we zoomed
into the previous panel's planet view.

CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT:
The background is the same dark radar screen from Panel 3, but
now zoomed in dramatically. The circular grid lines are more
visible. All other dots on the radar have faded to dim gray —
only the one signal remains bright. The composition should feel
like a "lock-on" moment — the radar found what it was looking
for.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
Colors: dark background (#0A0A14), the signal point is intense
neon green (#7FFF00) with a bright white (#FFFFFF) core. The
concentric rings fade from bright green to transparent. The
human silhouette is outlined in neon green with a soft glow.
All other elements are very dim gray (#1A1A1A).
Lighting: the signal point is the dominant light source — it
bleeds light outward. The silhouette glows from within. High
contrast between the single bright signal and the dark
surroundings. Mood: discovery, "eureka", the moment the radar
found its target.

STYLE AND AESTHETICS:
Pixel art style — clean blocky pixels, visible pixel grid,
no anti-aliasing, no smoothing. Limited color palette (max 5
colors — almost monochromatic green on black). The concentric
rings should look like pixel circles (slightly jagged edges,
as proper pixel art circles do). Inspired by target lock-on
screens from games like Metroid or Star Fox — that moment when
the reticle locks onto something important.

FORMAT: Single panel illustration, horizontal orientation
(1080 x 960 px). No text, no speech bubbles, no UI elements.
```

## Panel 5 · "Te encontré"

**Narrativa**: close-up de Spoky, sonrisa decidida, ojos brillantes, antena levantada.
**Texto en post-prod**: Spoky (burbuja de diálogo): "¡Te encontré!"
**Referencia de Spoky**: subir `spoky-frontal-canonico.png` como imagen de referencia.

```
MAIN SUBJECT:
Based on this character reference — a close-up portrait of Spoky,
the neon green pixel art alien. Head and upper torso visible,
filling most of the frame. Expression: determined but with NO
MOUTH, NO SMILE — emotion is conveyed ONLY through the eyes
and antennas, exactly as the reference image. Eyes slightly
narrowed with confidence, white cross-shaped highlights in both
eyes extra bright. Both zigzag antennas are perked up, angular
and sharp. One hand is raised, pointing forward toward the
viewer (toward the student).
Viewing angle: front-facing close-up, slightly from below
(low angle) to make Spoky look heroic and determined.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT add any mouth, smile, or facial expression
beyond the eyes. The character's face must match the reference
image exactly — large dark eyes with cross highlights are the
ONLY facial features. No mouth line, no teeth, no smile.

CONTEXT AND ENVIRONMENT:
Behind Spoky, the radar screen from the previous panels is
visible but out of focus — a blurred circle of green light in
the background. The cockpit interior is still dark but now feels
less lonely — there is purpose in the air. The green glow from
the radar illuminates Spoky from behind, creating a rim light
effect on his head and antennas.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
Colors: Spoky in full bright neon green (#7FFF00) with darker
green (#3D8B37) shading — must match the reference image exactly.
Background is dark (#0A0A14) with a soft green radar glow circle
behind Spoky's head (like a halo). The eyes are large, dark
(#1A1A2E) with prominent white (+) cross highlights.
Lighting: front-lit by the radar glow reflecting off the console
below (green uplight on chin/chest), plus rim light from behind
(radar screen). Eyes are the brightest point — the cross
highlights almost seem to sparkle.
Mood: determined, hopeful, "I found you" — the emotional
climax of the comic strip.

STYLE AND AESTHETICS:
Pixel art style — clean blocky pixels, visible pixel grid,
no anti-aliasing, no smoothing. Limited color palette (max 6
colors). This is the HERO SHOT of the comic — Spoky should look
as expressive and charismatic as possible using ONLY his eyes
and antennas. Inspired by character close-ups from Undertale
and Celeste. The low angle and rim lighting should make this
feel like the most visually striking panel of the five.

FORMAT: Single panel illustration, horizontal orientation
(1080 x 960 px). No text, no speech bubbles, no UI elements.
Leave space at the bottom-right area for a speech bubble to be
added in post-production (Canva).
```

## Ensamblaje final en Canva

Una vez aprobados los 5 paneles:

### Paso 1 — Crear lienzo vertical
- Canva → tamaño personalizado: **1080 × 4800 px** (5 paneles × 960 px)
- Fondo: negro (#0A0A0A) para separadores entre paneles

### Paso 2 — Apilar paneles
- Panel 1 arriba → Panel 5 abajo
- Separador entre paneles: 8-12 px de espacio negro

### Paso 3 — Añadir texto
| Panel | Tipo | Texto | Estilo sugerido |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Narración (caja) | "En algún lugar de la Tierra… algo cayó del cielo." | Fuente pixel (Press Start 2P o similar), blanco sobre fondo negro semi-transparente, esquina superior |
| 2 | Pensamiento (burbuja nube) | "La nave no puede despegar sin tripulación…" | Misma fuente, burbuja con borde pixelado |
| 3 | Narración (caja) | "El Radar de Potencial barrió el planeta entero…" | Misma fuente, esquina superior |
| 4 | Narración (caja) | "…y se detuvo en una señal." | Misma fuente, esquina inferior |
| 5 | Diálogo (burbuja clásica) | "¡Te encontré!" | Misma fuente, burbuja con cola apuntando a Spoky, bottom-right |

### Paso 4 — Exportar
- Formato: PNG
- Nombre: `E0-01-COM-la-senal-FINAL.png`
- Verificar peso ≤ 5 MB para WhatsApp
- Si supera 5 MB → exportar como JPG calidad 85%
